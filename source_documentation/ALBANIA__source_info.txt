SOURCE 2: Forebears.io — Albanian Forenames (Cumulative Population)
===========================================================
URL:            https://forebears.io/albania/forenames
Coverage:       121,969 unique forenames in Albania; 24 people per name on average
Data type:      Cumulative population data (not birth-year specific)
Years:          Not specified; reflects contemporary population records

File saved:     albania_forebears_top100.csv
Columns:        rank, name, incidence, frequency_ratio, gender_pct
Rows:           100 (top 100 of 121,969 unique forenames)

Notes:
  - Incidence = estimated total number of people in Albania bearing this name
  - Frequency ratio = approximately 1 in N people have this name
  - gender_pct = percentage of bearers who are the dominant gender
    (100% = exclusively one gender)
  - Nearly all top names are 100% gender-specific; exceptions: Denis (99% M),
    Andrea (99% M)
  - The forebears data reflects the full living population and thus strongly
    reflects names popular in the 1960s–1980s (communist era):
    Lindita, Arben, Mimoza, Agim, Fatmir are characteristic of that period
  - The 2019 INSTAT newborn data (Source 1) shows very different trends:
    Amelia, Ajla, Noel, Mateo — reflecting post-2000 Western name influence
  - The top forebears name Arben (13,278) was the all-time most popular name
    (1907-2017), peaking in 1972
  - Forebears does not offer CSV download; data extracted manually
  - Note: the forebears file uses "Gezim" for Gëzim (diacritic stripped)
    and "Aferdita" for Afërdita — original Albanian diacritics preserved in
    the CSV where possible but some may be missing

Download status: SUCCESS (top 100 extracted manually from forebears.io)

ALL-TIME NOTES (1907–2017 INSTAT data)
===========================================================
According to the Invest in Albania source (citing INSTAT 1907-2017 data):
  All-time top girl names: Lindita (502), Mimoza (427), Shpresa (346)
  All-time top boy names:  Arben (436), Gezim (334)
  Peak years: 1970-1972 for all of the above (communist-era naming trends)


===========================================================
LINGUISTIC NOTES
===========================================================
- Albanian is the sole member of its branch of Indo-European (no close relatives)
- Two main dialects: Gheg (northern, including Kosovo) and Tosk (southern)
- Standard Albanian (Tosk-based) uses Latin alphabet with diacritics: ë, ç
- Names often have distinct feminine forms (e.g., Ardian M / Ardianka F)
- Communist era (1944-1991) produced many invented Albanian-origin names
  (Lindita = "born of light", Mimoza = mimosa flower, Shpresa = "hope")
- Post-1991 trend: international names (Amelia, Noel, Mateo, Joel) increasingly
  popular for newborns
