SOURCE 1: Kemendagri / Dukcapil (Official Government Data)
===========================================================
Source:        Kementerian Dalam Negeri (Ministry of Home Affairs), Direktorat Jenderal
               Kependudukan dan Pencatatan Sipil (Dukcapil)
Announced by:  Wakil Menteri Dalam Negeri (Deputy Minister) Bima Arya Sugiarto
               via Instagram @bimaaryasugiarto, January 13, 2025
Years covered: 2024 (baby names registered in 2024); e-KTP all-time cumulative
Data type:     Top 10 baby names by gender (2024 births) + top 10 all-time names from e-KTP database
Format:        Only published as press release / infographic; no official download portal
Coverage:      National (all Indonesia)
Basis:         Civil registration records via NIK (Nomor Induk Kependudukan / National Identity Number)

Download status: NO DIRECT DOWNLOAD AVAILABLE from official source.
File saved:    indonesia_names_kemendagri_dukcapil_2024.csv
               (Manually reconstructed from authoritative press reports)

Notes:
- baby_2024 category = top 10 most-given names to babies born in 2024
- ektp_alltime category = top 10 most common names in the entire e-KTP population register (as of Jan 2025)
- Only top 10 per gender per category were released publicly
- Deeper data (top 100+, full frequency tables) not publicly released
