SOURCE: German municipal open data portals (no national registry exists)
Note: Germany has no centralized national given-name registry. Data is published by
individual Standesämter (registry offices) at municipal/city level.
Downloaded: 2026-05-01

=== BERLIN (most comprehensive; covers all 12 city districts) ===
Source: Berlin State Office for Citizens' and Civil Services (LABO), via BerlinOnline GmbH
URL: https://github.com/berlin/haeufige-vornamen-berlin
Portal: https://daten.berlin.de
Years: 2012–2023
Folder: BERLIN_2012-2023/
Structure: one subfolder per year; each subfolder has 12 CSV files (one per district).
    Districts: charlottenburg-wilmersdorf, friedrichshain-kreuzberg, lichtenberg,
    marzahn-hellersdorf, mitte, neukoelln, pankow, reinickendorf, spandau,
    steglitz-zehlendorf, tempelhof-schoeneberg, treptow-koepenick
Columns (2017+): vorname, anzahl, geschlecht (m/w), position (1/2/3...)
Columns (2012-2016): vorname, anzahl, geschlecht (no position field)
Note: "anzahl" counts are per district; aggregate across districts for city-level totals.
License: Creative Commons Attribution (CC BY)

=== ESSEN (North Rhine-Westphalia; city-level) ===
Source: Standesamt Stadt Essen (Essen Registry Office) — Department 33
URL: https://opendata.essen.de/dataset/vornamen
Years: 2009–2024
Folder: ESSEN_2009-2024/
Files: Vornamen_Essen_YYYY.csv (16 files, one per year)
Columns: anzahl, vorname, geschlecht, position
License: Data License Germany — Attribution 2.0 (dl-de/by-2-0)

=== COLOGNE / KÖLN (North Rhine-Westphalia; city-level) ===
Source: Standesamt Stadt Köln (Cologne Registry Office)
URL: https://www.offenedaten-koeln.de/dataset/vornamen
Years: 2010–2023
Folder: KOELN/
Files:
  - Vornamen_Koeln_2010-2018.csv    — combined time series 2010–2018
  - Vornamen_Koeln_2019-2022.csv    — combined time series 2019–2022
  - Vornamenstatistik_Koeln_2023.csv — single year 2023
Columns: anzahl, vorname, geschlecht, position (Namensposition)
License: Data License Germany — Zero — Version 2.0 (dl-de/zero-2-0)

RECOMMENDATION FOR RESEARCH USE:
Combine Berlin (large N, 12 districts, 2012-2023) as primary source.
Essen and Cologne serve as cross-validation and extend coverage to 2009.
All three cities are in western Germany. For regional diversity, add datasets
from daten.berlin.de (eastern Germany) and govdata.de for other states.
