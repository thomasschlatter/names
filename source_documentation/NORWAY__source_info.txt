Columns:        Fornavn (name code), then paired columns for each year 1880–2025:
                - PersonerProsent YYYY (% of births with this name)
                - Personer YYYY (absolute count)
Names:          1,974 distinct first names (prefixed "1" = female, "2" = male)
                Only names used by >= 200 persons in previous year are included.

Years covered:  1880–2025 (146 years)
Last updated:   2026-01-28

NOTES:
- Name codes starting with "1" are female names, "2" are male names.
- From 2021 onward, year = year of naming; before 2021, year = year of birth.
- Figures < 4 persons are not published (privacy protection).
- The API returns all data in a single wide-format CSV.
