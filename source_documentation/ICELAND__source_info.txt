NOTE ON ICELAND NAMING LAWS:
Iceland has strict naming laws enforced by the Icelandic Naming Committee
(Mannanafnanefnd). New names must be approved. The approved name list is
searchable online but not available as a bulk CSV download.
Names must be compatible with Icelandic grammar and orthography.
Both patronymics (e.g. Jónsson, Jónsdóttir) and given names are regulated.
