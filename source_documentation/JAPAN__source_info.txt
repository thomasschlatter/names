JAPAN BABY NAMES — DATA SOURCES
================================

FILES DOWNLOADED
----------------

1. Data_Baby_Names_Japan_2004-2018.xlsx
   Source: Meiji Yasuda Life Insurance annual survey (OSF deposit)
   OSF project DOI: https://doi.org/10.17605/OSF.IO/2WURJ
   Direct download: https://osf.io/download/cdt6j/
   Paper: Ogihara, Y. (2021). Baby names in Japan, 2004-2018: Common writings and their readings.
          BMC Research Notes, 13, 565.
          PMC: https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/pmc/articles/PMC7724864/
   Years: 2004-2018
   Format: Excel (.xlsx)
   Contents: Common kanji/kana writings of top baby names and their hiragana readings,
             by year and sex. Frequency counts and percentages included.
   Sample size: ~600-900 names per year (boys and girls separately)

2. Data_Baby_Names_Japan_2019-2024.xlsx
   Source: Meiji Yasuda Life Insurance annual survey (OSF deposit, follow-up)
   OSF project DOI: https://doi.org/10.17605/OSF.IO/BQUJN
   Direct download: https://osf.io/download/bkq5r/
   Paper: Ogihara, Y. (2025). Baby names in Japan, 2019-2024: Common writings and their readings.
          Data in Brief, 60, 111497.
          PubMed: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/40275978/
          PMC: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC12020897/
          ScienceDirect: https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S235234092500229X
   Years: 2019-2024
   Format: Excel (.xlsx)
   Contents: Common writings of baby names and their readings, generated from annual
             surveys on baby names conducted by Meiji Yasuda Life Insurance.
   Sample size: 4,335 total (2,115 boys, 2,220 girls) across 6 years;
                average ~723 per year

UNDERLYING DATA SOURCE
----------------------
Both datasets derive from Meiji Yasuda Life Insurance Company's annual survey
of baby names. The company collects names from its customers who have babies
each year. This is the standard academic source for Japanese baby names, as
there is no official government-published name frequency dataset in Japan.

NOTES
-----
- Data covers the top-ranked names only, not the full distribution.
- Both files together cover 2004-2024.
- A separate OSF deposit (https://osf.io/za6x7/) covers 1989-2003 (Ogihara 2022,
  BMC Research Notes), extending coverage back further if needed.
- The surveys are conducted annually; data is collected from Meiji Yasuda
  insurance customers (not a population census).
