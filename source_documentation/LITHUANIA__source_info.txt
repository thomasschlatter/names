== NOTES ON DATA ==
- All data originates from the Lithuanian Population Register (Gyventojų registras / GR)
- Maintained by VĮ Registrų centras, published via data.gov.lt
- Language family: Baltic (Indo-European) — Lithuanian names are highly distinctive
  (strong use of nominal suffixes: -as/-is for male, -a/-ė for female)
- Popular 2024 names: boys = Benas, Markas, Motiejus, Lukas, Adomas;
  girls = Amelija, Sofija, Emilija (from press reports)
