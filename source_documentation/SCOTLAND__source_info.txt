=== CELTIC/GAELIC RELEVANCE ===
Scotland is a major source of Scottish Gaelic (Gàidhlig) given names.
IMPORTANT CAVEAT: The NRS data does NOT separately flag Gaelic-origin names.
Scottish Gaelic names that appear in Scotland's top rankings include:
  Girls: Eilidh, Isla, Catriona, Morag, Mhairi/Mairi, Fiona, Seonaid
  Boys: Alasdair/Alastair, Fergus, Hamish, Calum, Ruaraidh, Iain, Euan, Cailean
The historical CSV (1974–2024) is the key file: it contains the full name universe
for all 51 years in a clean, ready-to-analyse format.
