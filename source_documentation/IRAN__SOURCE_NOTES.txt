IRAN — Given Names Data Sources
==================================
Retrieved: 2026-05-01

OFFICIAL SOURCE
---------------
Sazman-e Sabte Ahval (National Organization for Civil Registration / سازمان ثبت احوال کشور)
  URL: https://www.sabteahval.ir
  Status: Website connection refused at time of retrieval (likely geo-blocked or temporarily down)

  Sabteahval publishes annual lists of the most frequent newborn names, typically released
  through their spokesman and covered by Iranian news agencies. The data is released by year
  and usually covers the top 10-20 names for each sex, often with frequency counts.

  The name frequency portal tab: https://www.sabteahval.ir/default.aspx?tabid=1383
  (فراوانی نامگذاری — Naming Frequency) — connection refused from outside Iran.

  The interactive name system (سامانه تعاملی نام) at sahim.sabteahval.ir allows browsing
  of approved names by province and month, but appears to not offer bulk download.

SCI (Statistical Centre of Iran / مرکز آمار ایران / درگاه ملی آمار)
  URL: https://amar.org.ir
  Assessment: Publishes general demographic statistics; name frequency data is handled
  by Sabteahval, not SCI.

DATA COLLECTED
--------------
Year 1402 (first 4 months only, approx. April-July 2023):
  - Source: Deputy of Identity Documents Affairs, Civil Registration Organization
  - Reported via: baztabonline.com (https://baztabonline.com/news/...)
  - Top 9 girls + top 10 boys with exact counts
  - File: iran_popular_names_sabteahval_1402.csv
  - HTML archive: sabteahval_popular_names_1402_first4months.html

Year 1400 (annual rankings, approx. 2021-2022):
  - Source: Sabteahval spokesman Seyyed-Safi Abolorabi
  - Reported via: khabaronline.ir
  - Top 20 girls + top 20 boys (ranks only, no counts)
  - File: iran_popular_names_sabteahval_1400.csv

Year 1398-1399 (9-month period, approx. 2019-2020):
  - Source: Sabteahval spokesman Seifollah Aboutorabi
  - Reported via: Tehran Times (English) and aftabnews.ir
  - Total births: 913,345 (470,906 boys, 442,439 girls)
  - Top 10 names each sex (ranks, some with counts)
  - File: tehrantimes_popular_names_2019.html

NOTE ON COUNTS IN 1402 DATA
----------------------------
The counts for 1402 (e.g., Fatemeh 6,385; Amir Ali 5,217) represent registrations in
the FIRST FOUR MONTHS ONLY of 1402. Annual totals would be approximately 3x higher.
These are newborn registration counts, not population totals.
