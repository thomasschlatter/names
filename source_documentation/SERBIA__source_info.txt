== ANNUAL NEWBORN NAME DATA (no download; press reports only) ==

RZS annually publishes the most popular names for newborns. Data is released via press releases
at stat.gov.rs (section: Stanovništvo > Rođeni i umrli). No CSV/XLS download is offered.

Recent top newborn names (from RZS press reports):
- 2023: Boys = Luka, Vasilije, Lazar, Bogdan, Vuk; Girls = Sofija, Dunja, Mila, Teodora, Maša
- 2022: Boys = Luka (top), Bogdan, Vuk, Stefan, Aleksa; Girls = Sofija (top), Dunja, Mila
- Historical: Jelena was #1 female name for 19 years (1976–1994); Milica for 17 years (1995–2011)

Annual statistics source page:
   URL: https://www.stat.gov.rs/sr-latn/vesti/20240308-najcescaimenaiprezimena/?a=0&s=0503
   Also: https://popis2022.stat.gov.rs/en-us/5-vestisaopstenja/news-events/20240308-njcescaimenaiprezimena/

== NOTES ==
- Language family: Slavic (South Slavic) — Serbian uses both Cyrillic (primary official) and
  Latin (Latinica) script. The RZS publication uses Latin script for this dataset.
- The 2022 census PDF is the most comprehensive structured source available publicly.
- For machine-readable annual newborn name data, a direct data request to RZS may be needed.
- RZS contact: https://www.stat.gov.rs/en-us/o-nama/kontakt/
