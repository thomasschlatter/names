CONTENT OF EXCEL FILES:
Each XLSX contains ~16 sheets covering: total births, births by citizenship, fertility
rates, and — crucially — sheets with the most frequent given names for newborns by
sex (typically "Nomi maschili" and "Nomi femminili" sheets), with frequency counts
by region. Data source: "Iscritti in anagrafe per nascita" (Registry Office births).
