FILES COLLECTED (2026-05-01 — NEW ADDITIONS)
--------------------------------------------------------------------
forebears_greece_forenames_top1000.csv
  - 1,000 Greek names with absolute incidence counts and frequency ratios
  - Source: forebears.io/greece/forenames (genealogy/census aggregator)
  - Columns: rank, name, gender_inferred, pct_female, incidence, frequency_ratio,
             frequency_pct, source
  - Total coverage: 68,590 unique forenames in Greece; ~10.9M population base
  - Top names: Maria (519,581), Georgios (456,323), Dimitrios (359,849)
  - Gender inferred from gender-bar proportions (M/F/MF)
  - NOTE: Forebears aggregates multiple official sources (census, civil registry)
    but does not publish the exact year/edition. Use incidence ranks, not raw counts
    as authoritative population figures.

behindthename_greek_names.csv
  - 441 Modern Greek names (deduplicated across 2 pages)
  - Source: behindthename.com/names/usage/greek
  - Columns: name, gender (M/F/MF), name_key, language, source, has_frequency
  - NO frequency data; lexical/onomastic reference list only
  - Gender: M=masculine, F=feminine, MF=listed as both on the site

FILES COLLECTED (2026-05-01 — ORIGINAL BATCH)

foundalis_asep2008_greek_names.csv
  - 1,010 Greek names with frequency counts from ASEP civil service exam
  - Sample: ~97,072 individuals, autumn 2008
  - Columns: overall_rank, gender_rank_within_sex, name_greek, freq_in_sample,
             est_population_10M, gender_inferred, source
  - Source: https://www.foundalis.com/grk/EllinikaOnomata.html
  - NOTE: ASEP exam skewed female (~70% female applicants); freq counts for
    female names are inflated relative to male. Use for name list, not freq ratio.

elstat_popular_greek_names.csv
  - 80 names: top-10 all-ages population (with % for top 5) + top-30 per
    birth year from 2021 census
  - Data assembled from multiple ELSTAT-sourced articles (2019-2024)
  - Columns: name_greek, name_latin, gender, rank_population, pct_population,
             rank_births_2021, source
  - 1940 all-time top: Georgios 10.59%, Maria 9.48%
  - 2021 top: Georgios 7.58%, Maria 7.71%

greeknamedays_facebook_top30.csv
  - 30 Greek names (mixed gender) with frequency from 46,314 Facebook users
  - Period: 2009-2012. Source: greeknamedays.gr
  - Columns: name_greek, name_latin, gender, count_in_sample_46314, pct_in_sample

palaiologos2021_twitter_greek_male_names.csv
  - 11 male Greek names with % of male population
  - Source: Elias Palaiologos tweet (Oct 2021), citing ELSTAT 2011 study
  - Columns: name_greek, name_latin, rank, pct_of_male_population

philipperemy_facebook_greece_firstnames.csv
  - 15,626 names appearing in Facebook data (533M accounts) linked to Greece (GR)
  - Names with GR in top-10 country distribution. 9,552 have explicit GR rank.
  - Ranked 1-9552, plus 6,074 lower-frequency names without explicit rank
  - Source: philipperemy/name-dataset (Facebook leak dataset, ~2021)
  - Columns: name, rank_GR, prob_GR, gender_F, gender_M, gender_inferred
  - WARNING: Facebook data, not census. Includes diaspora. Many names are
    transliterations/variants (Γιώργος AND Giorgos both present).

sigpwned_wikipedia_greece_names.csv (embedded in sigpwned common-forenames-by-country.csv)
  - 21 rows: top-11 female + top-10 male, 2010, ranked only, no frequency
  - Source: Wikipedia "Lists of most common given names" (sigpwned/popular-names-by-country-dataset)

--------------------------------------------------------------------
NOTES ON GREEK NAME DATA
--------------------------------------------------------------------
- ELSTAT's Vital Statistics Survey has been conducted since 1956.
- Data on births transmitted to ELSTAT monthly from the Ministry of Interior
  since May 2013 (civil registration system).
- The 2021 Census recorded the 30 most frequent male/female names per birth
  year as a percentage of all children born.
- Most popular names (all ages): Georgios/Giorgos (male, ~10%),
  Maria (female, ~10%), followed by Giannis, Kostas, Nikos, Dimitris (male)
  and Eleni, Katerina, Vasiliki, Panagiota (female).
- Data request form for custom datasets: https://www.statistics.gr/en/statistical-data-request
