=== COVERAGE ===

MINIMUM THRESHOLD: 3+ occurrences per name per year
GEOGRAPHIC SCOPE: Republic of Ireland (not Northern Ireland)
LANGUAGE: Names registered can be Irish Gaelic, English, or other origin

=== CELTIC/GAELIC RELEVANCE ===
CRITICAL: Ireland is among the world's richest sources of native Irish Gaelic given names.
The CSO data includes both native-form Irish names (Aoife, Caoimhe, Saoirse, Fiadh,
Rían, Cillian, Ciarán, Seán, Eoin, Oisín, Tadhg) and anglicised forms (Niamh also
written as Nieve; Siobhán as Shivahn, etc.). The dataset is NOT pre-tagged by language
origin — all names are in the same table. As of 2024:
  - Top boys: Jack, Noah, Rián, Cillian, James (mix of English/Irish)
  - Top girls: Sophie, Éabha, Grace, Emily, Fiadh (mix; Éabha and Fiadh are Irish)
The complete 1964–2024 CSV (VSA50/VSA60) contains the full universe of all registered names.
