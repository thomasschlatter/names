Format:   Microsoft Excel (.xlsx), multiple worksheets
Content:  "Etunimien lukumäärät lajittelun mukaan" — First name counts sorted by frequency
          Includes separate tabs for men's and women's names, ranked by frequency
Data:     Counts of all persons with each given name in the population register
Source:   Population Information System (Väestötietojärjestelmä)
Updated:  February 3, 2026 (annual update)
License:  Open data (DVV open data license)

2024 highlights (from press release):
- Most popular new baby names in 2024: Olivia (girls), Oliver (boys)
- Previously: Aino (girls), Eino (boys) in prior year
- Data also covers Swedish-speaking and other-language-speaking children separately

Also available: surname data (sukunimitilasto) at same portal.
