SOURCE: INE — Instituto Nacional de Estadística (Spain)
URL: https://www.ine.es/dyngs/INEbase/operacion.htm?c=Estadistica_C&cid=1254736177009&menu=resultados&idp=1254735572981
Survey: "Apellidos y nombres más frecuentes" (Most frequent surnames and given names)
Downloaded: 2026-05-01

FILES:
- nombres_mas_frecuentes.xlsx
    Top 100 given names nationally + top 50 per province.
    Data: current resident population. Years: cumulative (no single year; reflects
    the Padrón Municipal snapshot, last update typically the prior January 1).

- nombres_por_fecha.xlsx
    Top 50 given names by decade of birth (national) + top 20 per province by decade.
    Useful for diachronic analysis of naming trends across birth cohorts.

- nombres_por_nacionalidades.xlsx
    Top 10 given names for the most-represented nationalities in Spain (national level).

- nombres_por_edad_media.xlsx
    ALL given names with frequency >= 20, with mean age by name.
    This is the most comprehensive file: full list, not just top-N.

- nomnac24.xlsx
    Top 100 names for newborns in 2024 (most recent annual cohort data).
    Prior years available at: https://www.ine.es/daco/daco42/nombyapel/nomnacXX.xlsx
    (replace XX with 02..24 for 2002–2024).
