URL:            https://forebears.io/armenia/forenames
Coverage:       37,710 unique forenames in Armenia; 77 people per name on average
Data type:      Cumulative population census (not birth-year specific)
Years:          Not specified; reflects contemporary population records

File saved:     armenia_forebears_top100.csv
Columns:        rank, name, incidence, frequency_ratio, gender_pct
Rows:           100 (top 100 of 37,710 unique forenames)

Notes:
  - Incidence = estimated total number of people in Armenia bearing this name
  - Frequency ratio = approximately 1 in N people have this name
  - gender_pct = percentage of bearers who are the dominant gender
    (100% = exclusively one gender; lower values = more unisex)
  - Top names Armen (50,911) and Anahit (50,609) reflect older generation
    prevalence; recent newborn data (Source 1) shows different top names
  - Most names are 100% gender-specific; exceptions include Karen (90% M),
    Nune (54% — borderline unisex), Narek (89% M)
  - Forebears does not offer CSV download; data extracted manually
  - Full dataset extends to rank 1000+ (rank 1000 = Yermonya, 213 incidences)

SCRIPT-WRITING NOTES
===========================================================
- Armenian is written in the Armenian alphabet (Հայկական այբուբեն), a unique
  script used exclusively for Armenian. All data in saved files uses romanized
  transliterations as published by official sources.
- Armenian is its own branch of Indo-European — not related to Iranian, Slavic,
  or any other branch. It constitutes a single-language branch.
- Eastern Armenian is the official language of the Republic of Armenia;
  Western Armenian is the diaspora variety.
- Common Armenian name-forming elements: -ik (diminutive), -uhi/-ine (feminine),
  -yan suffix in surnames (not given names)
