NOTES:
- Russia does not publish a national-level given names frequency dataset
- The Moscow portal (data.mos.ru) is the most authoritative open data source for Russian names
  but requires access from a non-blocked network (try VPN or download from Russia/EU server)
- Rosstat demographic yearbooks contain births counts but not name breakdowns
- The rulexicon RData file is the best currently-accessible Russian newborn names data
  (covers 2015-2020, from official Moscow ZAGS records)
