=== COVERAGE ===
COMBINED HISTORICAL: 1996–2024 (England + Wales, babynames1996to2024.xlsx)
WALES-ONLY TOP 100: 2019–2024 (annual files, Table 3 in each xlsx)
MINIMUM THRESHOLD: 3+ occurrences for full list (Table 6); top 100 for Wales-specific table
GEOGRAPHIC SCOPE: Births registered in Wales (mother's usual residence in Wales)

=== IMPORTANT LIMITATION ===
The Wales-only data (Table 3) contains only the TOP 100 names per year, not the full name
universe. The full name universe (Table 6) is England+Wales combined only.
There is no publicly available full-universe Wales-only breakdown.
For frequencies of Welsh-specific names below rank 100, use the England+Wales combined data
(babynames1996to2024.xlsx or Table 6 in annual files) and note that Welsh names will be
proportionally underrepresented in the aggregate.

=== CELTIC/BRYTHONIC RELEVANCE ===
Wales is the primary source of Brythonic Celtic (Welsh-language) given names.
Welsh names appearing in Wales Top 100:
  Girls (2021-2024): Seren, Nia, Ffion, Cadi, Elen, Alys, Lowri, Cerys, Eira, Gwenllian
  Boys (2021-2024): Osian, Rhodri, Gethin, Tomos, Huw, Cai, Emrys, Macsen, Llŷr, Ifor
The Wales-specific Table 3 in annual files is the key resource for identifying which
specifically Welsh-language names are in use and at what frequency.
